RESEARCH CONNECTION

How the experiment maps to BDH / BDH-CQ

PUBLISHED

BDH: synaptic memory

The BDH paper describes a brain-inspired architecture in which inference-time working memory relies on synaptic plasticity and Hebbian learning. Pathway's “Equations of Reasoning” explain local read/write dynamics and synaptic state.

Read BDH paper →
PUBLISHED

BDH-CQ: recurrent latent reasoning

BDH-CQ uses inference-time inputs to update recurrent memory, then performs iterative computation in latent space without verbalizing intermediate reasoning. This is the research-side analogue of our visible-state lesson.

Read BDH-CQ paper →
PATHWAY RESEARCH

Equations of Reasoning

The Pathway article describes four local rounds: memory read, synaptic reweighting, gated neuron dynamics, and state update. Our toy system deliberately abstracts this into a smaller matrix-memory experiment.

Read research article →
Evidence boundary: MemoryLab is an independent educational toy system. It is not an official BDH or BDH-CQ implementation and its results must not be presented as BDH benchmark results.
